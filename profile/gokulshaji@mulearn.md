# About Me

I am **Gokul Shaji**, a third-year B.Tech Computer Science student at **St. Joseph's College of Engineering and Technology (SJCET), Palai**. I currently work as a **Backend Developer Intern at the μLearn Foundation**, where I contribute to building scalable backend systems and real-world software solutions.

My primary domains are **Full-Stack Development** and **Artificial Intelligence**. I enjoy solving real-world problems through technology and constantly challenge myself by learning new frameworks, tools, and development practices. Alongside my internship, I actively practice Data Structures and Algorithms and build personal projects that improve productivity and user experience.

One of my current projects is **FocusLock**, an Android application built using **Kotlin** that helps users stay productive by locking selected applications during scheduled time periods. Once a lock is activated, users cannot access the chosen apps until the scheduled duration ends, helping reduce distractions and improve focus.

I joined **μFIFA** to collaborate with passionate builders, learn from an amazing community, contribute to impactful projects, and push myself to become a better software engineer. By the end of the tournament, I want to strengthen my technical skills, contribute to open source, and build projects that create real value.
